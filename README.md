# Artvas

Artvas is a free all-in-one creative studio that runs in the browser. It has a design editor for social posts, posters and presentations, a timeline video editor for TikTok, Instagram Reels, Xiaohongshu (RED), Facebook and YouTube, and AI image and video generation.

## Video editor

- **Platform presets**: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Xiaohongshu / RED (3:4), Facebook Reels and feed (4:5), YouTube Shorts, YouTube (16:9) and square.
- **Starter layouts**: Hook + captions, Vlog intro, Product promo, How-to steps, RED note cover, and Quote / lyrics.
- **Timeline**: a main video track, overlay tracks for text and stickers, and audio tracks. Drag clips to reorder them, drag edges to trim, split at the playhead (S), duplicate, delete and undo/redo. Zoom the timeline by pinching with two fingers, with Ctrl + scroll (or a trackpad pinch), or with the zoom slider; **Fit** shows the whole video.
- **Clips**: speed (0.25×–3×), volume and mute, fill frame or fit with a blurred background, 8 filters plus brightness, contrast and saturation, and a "clip motion" effect per clip.
- **Text and stickers**: 9 text styles (title, caption, outline, highlight, neon, lower third…), emoji stickers and the 3D graphics library. Drag them on the video to move them and drag the corner to resize. Each has in and out animations (fade, pop, slide, zoom, typewriter).
- **Music library (Artvas Music)**: 20 styles of original, royalty-free music composed in your browser: lo-fi, pop, hip-hop, trap, house, cinematic, corporate, ambient, sad piano, happy strum, synthwave, funk, reggaeton, drum & bass, suspense, kids, holiday, jazz, summer vlog and dreamy pop. Preview, make new variations and set the length (fit to video, 15, 30 or 60 s).
- **Sound effects**: 20 synthesized effects (whoosh, pop, ding, notification, camera, boom, riser, glitch, coin, success, laser, typing, drum roll, ta-da…).
- **Text to speech**: fast online neural voices in 17 languages, including Mandarin (Mainland, Taiwan and regional dialects), Cantonese, Malay, Indonesian, Tamil (Malaysia), Thai, Vietnamese, Filipino, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Arabic and English (US, UK, Singapore, India). They are generated by the Artvas server (`POST /api/tts`, using Microsoft Edge's free Read Aloud voices via [msedge-tts](https://www.npmjs.com/package/msedge-tts)), so they work on your own deployment, not on static hosting. There are also 27 private English voices ([Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX), Apache-2.0) that run in your browser. All voices have adjustable speed and optional matching captions timed per sentence.
- **Audio**: add music and voice-overs with volume and fade in/out. Clip sound is mixed in automatically.
- **Designs as slides**: turn any Artvas design into a video slide, one clip per page.
- **AI**: generate image clips, or video clips with Seedance, Wan, Kling, PixVerse or Veo 3, using your own Muapi key.
- **Video templates**: 35 Artvas templates (photo dump, beat sync, glitch intro, POV, GRWM, story time, year recap, day in my life, travel diary, recipe, OOTD, before/after, glow up, gym progress, top 3 countdown, product launch, flash sale, home tour, lyrics, RED note and more). Pick your photos and videos and they fill the slots, with cuts, effects, transitions and animated text already set up.
- **More sizes**: phone full-screen (19.5:9, iPhone 15/16, iPhone Pro Max, Android 20:9, QHD+), vertical HD and 4K, 4:5, 3:4, 2:3, 4K UHD, cinematic 21:9 and 4:3, plus any custom size up to 4096 px.
- **Video effects** (Effects tab): 38 effects in five groups, each previewed with a thumbnail. Motion (shake, beat zoom, slow zoom, swing, zoom blur, motion trail), Light (flash beat, strobe, dreamy glow, neon bloom, light leak, lens flare, rainbow, focus in), Retro (VHS, old film, film grain, REC camera, pixelate, RGB split, glitch, negative beat), Particles (snow, rain, hearts, confetti, sparkles, bokeh, bubbles, petals, fireworks) and Frame (cinema bars, vignette, mirror, kaleidoscope, split ×4, split ×9, countdown 3-2-1). An effect is a layer on its own timeline row: it applies to everything under it, and you can drag, trim, split, duplicate it or change its strength.
- **Body effects** (Effects tab): 13 effects that find the person in the video and draw around them: neon outline, rainbow aura, fire aura, body sparkles, clones, echo trail, body glitch, silhouette flash, background blur, color pop, spotlight, neon background and X-ray. Person detection uses Google's MediaPipe selfie segmenter (Apache-2.0); the 250 KB model ships in `public/models/`, and the MediaPipe runtime loads from jsDelivr the first time.
- **Transitions** (Transitions tab, or the ＋ between two clips on the timeline): 23 transitions with picture previews: crossfade, dip to black, flash, slide, push up, push down, zoom, zoom blur, wipe, diagonal wipe, circle, heart, spin, rotate zoom, flip, doors open, blinds, whip pan, pixelate, RGB split, shake cut, glitch and blur. Each plays a quick preview when you pick it. Set the length, use one everywhere, or mix them at random.
- **Crop and position**: zoom, move, rotate 90° or to any angle, and flip any clip. You can also replace a clip and add a freeze frame.
- **Masks**: circle, rectangle (rounded), heart, star, split line and mirror masks on clips and picture-in-picture, with soft edges, invert, position and rotation.
- **Blend modes and opacity**: 16 blend modes for overlays (multiply, screen, overlay, soft light, difference…) and opacity for clips.
- **Voice changer**: chipmunk, deep, robot, echo, telephone, megaphone, monster, cave, radio and underwater for voice-overs and clip sound. The voice keeps its timing.
- **Extract audio** from any video clip onto its own audio track.
- **Reverse** any video clip (up to 60 s), with its sound reversed too.
- **Speed curves**: Montage, Hero, Bullet, Jump cut, Flash in, Flash out, or your own curve (0.1×–10×) with draggable points.
- **Auto beat sync**: detect the beats in a music track, see beat marks on the timeline, cut your clips to every 1, 2 or 4 beats in one tap, and snap clip edges to beats.
- **Stabilize** shaky clips (low, medium or high strength).
- **Stock videos and music**: search free, openly licensed videos (Wikimedia Commons) in the Media tab and music (Openverse) in the Audio tab. Each item keeps its credit (title, author, licence).
- **Filters and adjustments**: 26 filters (teal & orange, golden hour, noir, cinematic, cyber…) plus brightness, contrast, saturation, temperature, tint, fade, vignette and sharpen.
- **Picture-in-picture**: put videos or photos on top of the main video, with green-screen (chroma key) removal, volume, circle crop and rotation.
- **Keyframes**: animate the position, size and opacity of text, stickers and overlays between keyframes.
- **Captions**: automatic captions from speech in your clips, picture-in-picture videos and voice-over tracks (Whisper, running in the browser). It detects the spoken language (English, Chinese, Malay, Indonesian, Tamil, Thai and more) and removes the repeated phrases speech models sometimes invent. Choose 2–6 words per caption or full lines. You can also get captions from any other video or audio file without adding it, write captions from a typed script, and pick from 5 caption styles.
- **Download captions**: save them as subtitles (.srt, for YouTube, Facebook, TikTok Studio and video players) or as a text transcript (.txt), or copy the text.
- **Import from a link**: paste a direct link to a video, photo or audio file (the Artvas server fetches it, up to 200 MB). Links to pages on social apps aren't supported; save the video from the app first.
- **Export**: MP4 (or WebM) at 480p up to full size, with mixed audio. Projects and media are saved in your browser (IndexedDB).

## Features

- **Start screen**: 18 size presets (Instagram, Story, YouTube, Presentation, A4 poster, business card and more), custom sizes, your saved designs (open, duplicate, delete), and opening a `.json` project file.
- **Templates**: 62 Artvas templates in 10 formats. The 43 trending designs (23 Instagram posts, 14 YouTube thumbnails, 4 Stories and 2 presentations) use current styles: aurora gradients, film grain, editorial serif and bold sans type, Y2K chrome, bento grids, sticker collages, neon glow and glass cards. There are also posters, business cards, invitations, a resume, logos and Facebook covers. You can search them and filter by category or format, and each one scales to any canvas size.
- **Photo placeholders**: dashed frames in templates. Select one and upload a photo, or click any image in Uploads, Photos or your Brand Kit logos, and it fills the frame cropped to fit. You can also add your own placeholders from the Elements tab.
- **Text**: heading, subheading and body presets, styled font combinations, and 29 fonts. Curve any text into an arc with the Curve slider. You can set bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, alignment, uppercase or lowercase, line height, letter spacing, highlight, outline, shadow and glow.
- **Elements**: a Canva-style library with search and "See all" views. It has 940+ glossy 3D graphics (Microsoft Fluent Emoji 3D, MIT), 930+ colourful illustrations (Google Noto Emoji, Apache-2.0) and 2,000+ recolourable icons (Lucide, ISC), all organised by category. There are also shapes and lines, photo frames (circle, arch, heart, star, polaroid, phone…), photo grids and collages, editable charts (bar, horizontal bar, line, donut, pie, progress ring), editable tables, gradient shapes and stickers. The artwork is bundled in `public/elements/` (rebuild it with `scripts/build-elements.py`).
- **Magic Write**: free AI writing in the Text tab. Describe what you need, pick a tone (friendly, professional, fun, persuasive) and a language (English, Chinese, Malay, Tamil, Indonesian), and add a headline or paragraph. It uses the free Pollinations text service through the Artvas server (`POST /api/free-text`).
- **QR codes**: make a QR code for any link or text in the Elements tab, in your own colours. Select it later to change the link.
- **Crop**: crop any photo by dragging the crop box (mouse or touch) or with exact sliders, in free, original, 1:1, 4:5, 16:9 or 9:16 shapes.
- **Duotone and photo colours**: six duotone presets or your own two colours, and the main colours of a photo shown as swatches in every colour picker.
- **Align and space evenly**: align selected elements to each other and space three or more of them evenly.
- **Present**: full-screen slideshow with animations, fade/slide/zoom transitions, autoplay, a laser pointer, speaker notes and a presenter view (current and next slide, notes, timer) in a second window.
- **Share and edit together**: copy a view link or an edit link. People on the same edit link see each other's changes live, with named cursors and avatars. Shared designs are stored on the Artvas server in `DATA_DIR` (default `./data`); on Railway, mount a volume at `/data` and set `DATA_DIR=/data` so they survive redeploys.
- **Free stock photos**: search openly licensed photos (Openverse, Wikimedia Commons) in the Photos tab. Optional: set `PEXELS_API_KEY` or `PIXABAY_API_KEY` to use those libraries instead.
- **Uploads**: upload images, drag and drop them onto the canvas, or paste them from the clipboard. Your recent uploads are kept.
- **Photos**: free stock photos (Lorem Picsum). Add a photo to the page or use it as the background.
- **Draw**: freehand pen and eraser with adjustable size and color.
- **Background**: solid colors, gradient presets, custom gradients (two colors and an angle), and background images.
- **Layers**: drag to reorder, show or hide, lock, and delete.
- **Edit panel**: fill and gradient fill, border (solid or dashed), corner radius, transparency, blend modes, shadow, align to page, exact X/Y/W/H/rotation, flip, and bring to front or send to back.
- **Image adjustments**: brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, blur and pixelate, plus filters (grayscale, sepia, vintage, polaroid and more). You can also replace an image.
- **Pages**: add, duplicate, reorder and delete pages.
- **Smart guides**: objects snap to the page center and edges.
- **Undo and redo, copy and paste, duplicate, group and ungroup, lock, and a right-click menu.**
- **Resize**: change a design to another size, either in place or as a resized copy.
- **Download**: PNG (with an optional transparent background), JPG, SVG, a multi-page PDF, or a `.json` project file. You can export at 0.5× to 3× size.
- **My templates**: save any design (one page or all pages) as your own template with a name and category. Templates fit any design size when used. You can use them from the editor or start a new design from the home page, and rename, delete, export and import them (a `.json` file, so you can move them to another browser or computer).
- **AI Image**: **Free AI** is the default and needs no key. It uses the Pollinations.ai community service through Artvas's server, is limited to about one image every 15 seconds and adds a small watermark. Or generate images with 10+ premium models (Flux, Nano Banana, Seedream, Imagen 4, Ideogram, Midjourney, GPT Image…) through [Muapi.ai](https://muapi.ai), using your own API key. The approach is based on the MIT-licensed [Open Higgsfield AI](https://github.com/sunnychase/open-higgsfield-ai). You can pick a style and an image shape. Results go onto the page or into the selected photo spot, and **Generate photos for this page** fills every photo spot in a template using prompts written for that template. Your key is stored only in your browser. On the Artvas server, requests go through a small relay (`/api/muapi/*`) that passes your key through and never stores it.
- **Background remover**: AI background removal that runs privately in the browser. With a GPU it uses [BiRefNet_lite](https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/BiRefNet_lite-ONNX) (MIT, about 115 MB); otherwise it uses [ormbg](https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/ormbg-ONNX) (Apache-2.0, about 44 MB). The model is downloaded once and then cached. There is also a "remove a color" tool for logos and flat backgrounds, and you can restore the original image.
- **Animations**: 13 element animations (fade, rise, drop, slide, zoom, pop, spin, bounce, typewriter, blink, pulse, wiggle), whole-page presets with staggered timing, per-element duration and delay, page duration, and a live preview.
- **Animated downloads**: an animated GIF, or an MP4 video (WebM in browsers that can't record MP4). Every page plays in order.
- **Brand Kit**: brand colors (shown in every color picker and applied to a page in one click), heading and body fonts, custom font upload (.ttf/.otf/.woff), and logos.
- **Version history**: a version is saved automatically every 3 minutes while you edit. You can also save named versions and restore any of the last 30.
- **Autosave**: designs are saved automatically in the browser (IndexedDB).
- **Fast start**: fonts load only when a design uses them. If Artvas is open in another tab and blocks saved designs from loading, the app still opens within a few seconds and shows a banner asking you to close the other tab and reload.

## Run locally

```bash
npm install
npm run dev        # development server at http://localhost:5173
npm run build      # production build into dist/
npm start          # serve dist/ on $PORT (default 3000)
```

## Deploy on Railway

1. Go to [railway.com](https://railway.com) → **New Project** → **Deploy from GitHub repo** → choose this repository.
2. Railway reads `railway.json`. It installs packages, builds with `npm run build`, starts with `npm start`, and checks health at `/health`.
3. Open the service → **Settings** → **Networking** → **Generate Domain** to get a public URL.

You don't need any environment variables. Railway sets `PORT` automatically.

## Deploy on Render (alternative)

[![Deploy to Render](https://render.com/images/deploy-to-render-button.svg)](https://render.com/deploy?repo=https://github.com/ZENSEE1314/Canva-free)

Render reads `render.yaml` (free web service, branch `ccr-8badd3cf-mv0e8o`). Free services sleep after 15 minutes without visits, so the first visit after that takes about a minute to wake up.

## Tech

Vanilla JS, [Fabric.js](https://fabricjs.com) 6, jsPDF, gifenc and Vite, served by a small Express server. The background remover loads [transformers.js](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers.js) from a CDN only when it is first used.
